# ---- Players page ----
players.title = MLB Players
players.lede = Every regular in MLB, ranked against the rest by Impact: the runs he has been worth this season beyond an average regular in his job.
players.note = Impact is runs above average, from the box scores of the regular season. A hitter is measured against regulars at his position on what he does at the plate: 0.54 runs for a single, double or triple (a box score does not say which), 1.40 for a home run, 0.31 for a walk, and 0.26 lost for an out. A pitcher is measured against regular starters or relievers on the earned runs he gives up for the outs he gets. It cannot see fielding or baserunning, and it does not adjust for the ballpark or the opponent. On-base percentage and slugging are ESPN's.
players.none_yet = No box scores yet this season. Players appear here after the first games.
players.back = All players
players.career_note = Each season's line is ESPN's, with a line for every team he has played for. It is read again after each of his team's games, so this season's line can be a game behind.
players.career_wait = His earlier seasons have not been read yet. They appear here within a day or two.
