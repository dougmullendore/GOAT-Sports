# ---- Players page ----
players.title = Top Players
players.lede = Every regular on a ranked team, ranked against the rest by Impact per game: the points he adds per game over an average top-25 regular at his position.
players.title_fbs = FBS Players
players.lede_fbs = Every regular in FBS, ranked against the rest by Impact per game: the points he adds per game over an average FBS regular at his position.
players.note = Impact compares each player only with regulars at his position on this week's top 25 teams, from the box scores. It cannot see blocking, coverage or who was on the field, it does not adjust for the opponent, and offensive linemen have no numbers to rate. Quarterbacks touch the ball most, so they fill the top and the bottom of the list.
players.note_fbs = Impact compares each player with every regular at his position in FBS, from the box scores. It cannot see blocking, coverage or who was on the field, and it does not adjust for the opponent, so players on weaker schedules can rate higher here than in the Top 25 view. Offensive linemen have no numbers to rate.
players.back = All players
players.career_note = Each season's line is ESPN's, with a line for every school he has played for. It is read again after each of his team's games, so this season's line can be a game behind.
players.career_wait = His earlier seasons have not been read yet. A few thousand players' careers are read on each update, the ranked teams' first, so they appear here within a day or two.
